---
title: Protected pages
description: Put individual pages behind a reading PIN — share docs with your users without publishing them to competitors.
---

Sometimes a page is for *your users*, not the whole internet: integration
details, roadmap notes, pricing internals. Protected pages stay on your docs
site with the same URL, sidebar entry, and layout — but the content only
renders after the visitor enters a **reading PIN** you control. Hand the PIN
to customers and partners; everyone else (competitors included) sees a lock
screen.

## Set the reading PIN

The PIN is a site-wide secret, separate from the admin PIN — it grants
*reading only*, never editing:

```bash
wrangler secret put READER_PIN
```

`ADMIN_SECRET` must also be set (it signs the unlock cookie — see
[standalone auth](/docs/standalone-auth)). With `READER_PIN` unset, protected
pages fail closed: only signed-in team members can read them.

## Protect a page

In the editor, use the **Protect** toggle next to the page title area — it
marks the page and Publish commits the change. Or by hand, add one line of
frontmatter:

```mdx
---
title: Enterprise rollout guide
description: For customers only.
protected: true
---
```

Signed-in admins and editors always see protected pages without a PIN.

## What readers see

The page keeps its title and description, but the body is replaced by a PIN
prompt. A correct PIN sets a cookie that lasts 30 days in that browser, then
the page renders normally. Changing `READER_PIN` (or `ADMIN_SECRET`)
invalidates every unlocked browser at once — that's your revocation lever.

## How it stays sealed

A protected page isn't just hidden client-side:

- Its **static build contains only the lock screen** — never the body. The
  content is served exclusively by an API that verifies the reader cookie on
  the server, so nothing confidential leaves your Worker without it.
- It's **out of the search index** — findable by title, but its content is
  never indexed.
- It's **absent from `llms-full.txt` and the `/llms.mdx` markdown mirrors**,
  the machine-readable exports agents use.

<Callout title="What stays visible">
  The page's title and description still appear in the sidebar, search
  results, and the lock screen itself — pick them accordingly. The reading
  PIN is one shared secret for the whole site, not per-page passwords.
</Callout>
